itsRide Mobile Rate & Quote Calculator V1.4

WHAT CHANGED
- Distance prices updated: Sedan $65 up to 25 km, SUV $80 up to 25 km and Luxury SUV $100 up to 25 km.
- For trips over 25 km: Sedan $2.25/km, SUV $2.85/km and Luxury SUV $3.00/km.
- Distance Pricing now calculates driving kilometres from the FROM and TO addresses when Google Maps is connected.
- Driving kilometres remain internal and are excluded from customer preview, WhatsApp text, PNG, JPG and PDF.
- Manual Base Fare still supports negotiated or special pricing.
- Round Trip doubles the one-way base fare.
- 171 official routes and official-route prices are preserved.
- Airport fee, Meet & Greet, waiting, tolls, parking, gratuity and HST remain separate line items when used.

GITHUB UPDATE
1. Open the existing itsride-mobile-quote repository.
2. Upload/replace ALL files from this V1.3 package in the repository root.
3. Commit changes to main.
4. Wait for GitHub Pages deployment.
5. Open https://itsridelimo.github.io/itsride-mobile-quote/
6. Refresh once. If an older Home Screen version remains, close it and reopen after the web page shows V1.4.

PRICING WORKFLOW
Official Route Rate: select saved route and vehicle.
Distance Pricing: enter FROM and TO; Google Maps calculates the internal one-way driving kilometres.
Manual Base Fare: enter FROM, TO and negotiated one-way fare.
